Explore Jakarta

Jakarta, the capital city of Indonesia, is a dynamic metropolis that blends tradition and modernity. It is known for its bustling streets, historic landmarks, and a diverse population that represents the cultural richness of the nation. As a business and political hub, Jakarta also offers a wide range of entertainment, shopping, and culinary experiences.

  • Popular Attractions: National Monument (Monas), Kota Tua, Ancol Dreamland
  • Best Time to Visit: June to September
  • Local Cuisine: Soto Betawi, Kerak Telor, Gado-Gado
